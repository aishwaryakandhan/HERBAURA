Hair Oil Care Routine

1. Choose the Right Hair Oil

Different hair oils can be included in different hair care routines. Coconut oil, amla oil, bhringraj oil, hibiscus oil and onion oil are popular choices for herbal-inspired hair care.

2. Apply Oil Gently

Apply a suitable amount of oil gently to the scalp and hair. Avoid using excessive force while massaging your scalp.

3. Give Your Hair Some Time

After applying hair oil, allow it to remain for some time according to your personal routine. You can then wash your hair with a suitable shampoo.

4. Do Not Overuse Oil

Using a large amount of oil does not necessarily provide better results. Use an amount that is comfortable for your hair and scalp.

5. Follow a Regular Routine

Hair oiling can be included as part of a regular self-care routine. Consistency and gentle hair care habits can make the routine easier to maintain.
