Simple Hair Care Tips

1. Keep Your Hair Clean

Keeping your hair and scalp clean is an important part of a regular hair care routine. Choose a suitable shampoo and wash your hair according to your personal routine and hair needs.

2. Use Hair Oil Regularly

Hair oiling can be included as a simple self-care routine. Coconut oil, amla oil, bhringraj oil and other herbal-inspired oils can be used as part of regular hair care.

3. Be Gentle With Your Hair

Avoid pulling or roughly brushing your hair. Handle wet hair gently and use a suitable comb to reduce unnecessary hair breakage.

4. Maintain a Simple Routine

A consistent routine can make hair care easier. Depending on your hair type, your routine may include cleansing, conditioning and occasional oiling.

5. Choose Products Carefully

When selecting hair care products, check the ingredients and choose products that suit your individual hair and scalp needs.
